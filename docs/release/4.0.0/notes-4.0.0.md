## Pluck Studio 4.0

- **Deliver:** review links for clients, hosted on your Mac or NAS, with comments on the frame, approvals, deadlines and markers back in DaVinci Resolve.
- **Edit Anywhere:** edit in DaVinci Resolve from anywhere on proxies, and render on the Mac with the originals. Connects with a one-time invitation link, no account or open ports.
- **Delivery Check with brand books:** every export checked against your brand book before it goes out.
- **New editing tools:** Sync Cameras, Fit Music, Social Cutdowns, Thumbnail Maker, keyframes and transitions.
- **A deeper Photo page:** AI Remove, Liquify, HDR, panorama and focus-stack merges, Super Resolution, Auto Cull, lens profiles, tethered shooting, History, Map and People.
- **A new look:** every page redesigned, a Finder-style Library and a new app icon.

### Bug fixes and improvements

- Security fixes across sharing and remote editing: links from other devices are checked, received files are quarantined, and the updater refuses older versions.
- Edits are saved on quit, and the Library follows files you rename or move.
- Offload skips macOS's hidden ._ files.
- Dehaze no longer makes halos; AI Remove no longer comes back black on the first try.
- The Photo page stays fast over a long session, and shows previews at once.
- Photo History is kept between sessions.
- Cull shows a true 100 % and focus peaking.
- Tools no longer clip at the smallest window size.
- ⌘F searches on every Studio page.
- Space plays a raw clip the first time it opens.
- The Logo Animator preview is sharp.
- Opening Pluck from the Dock or Spotlight shows its window at once, also with Start in menu bar on.
- A Dock click always brings the main window back.
- Translations for everything new.
- Client uploads that were half done before 4.0 start again from the beginning once.

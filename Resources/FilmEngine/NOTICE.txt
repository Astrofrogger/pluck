Pluck Studio's film simulation runs spektrafilm by Andrea Volpato:
https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm

spektrafilm is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3
(https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html). Its film and paper profiles are licensed under
CC BY-SA 4.0. Pluck doesn't include spektrafilm: it downloads spektrafilm and its Python
libraries only when you choose to, into its own folder, and runs it as a separate program.

pluck_film.py, the small script Pluck uses to talk to spektrafilm, is licensed under the
GNU GPL version 3 as well.

AI denoise (optional download)
SCUNet by Kai Zhang et al. (https://github.com/cszn/SCUNet), Apache License 2.0; weights from
https://github.com/cszn/KAIR. Run with spandrel (https://github.com/chaiNNer-org/spandrel), MIT
License, and PyTorch (BSD-style license).

pluck-spektra (Contents/Helpers/pluck-spektra)
Pluck's film engine, a separate program under the GNU GPL version 3 or later (COPYING). It is
built from darktable's native port of spektrafilm (https://github.com/darktable-org/darktable,
Copyright (C) 2026 darktable developers) with a driver following darktable's spektrafilm module.
Its source is in Pluck's repository, Helpers/PluckSpektra. The film and paper data is
downloaded from https://github.com/darktable-org/darktable-spektrafilm, CC BY-SA 4.0
